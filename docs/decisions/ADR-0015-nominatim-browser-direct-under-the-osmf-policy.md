# ADR-0015: Nominatim: browser-direct and submit-only, under the OSMF usage policy

- Status: Accepted (implementation); production use awaits maintainer question 1
- Date: 2026-09-24 (records D97, taken at M4 on 2026-09-09, and the M6 CSP and test additions)

## Context

The brief specifies the place search (OBS-4 [M], l.193): OpenStreetMap Nominatim "called from the browser and never from the API", explicit submit only, one request in flight, at least 1 s between requests, `format=jsonv2`, `limit=5`, `accept-language` set to the UI language, the attribution "© OpenStreetMap contributors", and the base URL, enabled flag, optional `email` and attribution served by `/meta.geocoder` so the service "can be switched or disabled without redeploying the frontend"; the data-sources row (l.318) repeats the policy points and states "never cached or proxied by the API"; the pitfalls (l.554) note that browsers cannot set `User-Agent`, so identification relies on the `Referer` through `<meta name="referrer" content="strict-origin-when-cross-origin">`.

The OSMF Nominatim usage policy (<https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/>, clauses quoted verbatim in `docs/plan.md` section 11, question 1) is a licensing and terms-of-use constraint and therefore sits at the first rank of the brief's precedence (l.11: "legal and licensing constraints > security and privacy > api_contract > architecture > [M] functional requirements"). It states an "absolute maximum of 1 request per second", requires "a valid HTTP Referer or User-Agent identifying the application", says "If at all possible, set up a proxy and also enable caching of requests", forbids client-side auto-complete, and in its "Usage in LLMs" section allows use only "where the application developer has made a deliberate, informed decision to use it and is directly responsible for complying with this policy", not as a generic geocoding service built into or generated by no-code, low-code or vibe-coding platforms. This project was assembled with an LLM under the maintainer's direction, so that clause applies to the maintainer, not to the agent. The tension between "never proxied" (l.193, l.318) and "set up a proxy if at all possible" (the policy) cannot be resolved by the agent: it is recorded as maintainer question 1 (raised before M4, updated at M4 with the verbatim clauses) and as plan risk R23.

## Options considered

1. Browser-direct, as specified by l.193 and l.318. Chosen for the implementation: it is what the brief specifies and every policy point the brief lists is implemented (rate, identification, attribution, no auto-complete, switchable). Whether browser-direct use of the public instance is the maintainer's deliberate, informed decision is question 1.
2. A caching proxy under `/api/v1/geocode` in the API. Not implemented: it contradicts l.193 and l.318 and the closed route list of the api_contract; it would also have to avoid storing requests (l.275). It remains the alternative offered in question 1 and would be an `api_version` change.
3. A self-hosted Nominatim or another provider. Available without code: `/meta.geocoder.url` switches the base URL (`SKYAPI_GEOCODER_URL`), and since M6 the CSP `connect-src` names the same origin through `SKY_GEOCODER_ORIGIN` (ADR-0018, backlog B-85), so a switch is a configuration change on both tiers.
4. No place search. Rejected: OBS-4 is [M]; the disable toggle and `/meta.geocoder.enabled` already let an operator turn it off.

Wording rule for every document: the design follows l.193 and l.318 as specified; the OSMF policy is a licensing constraint of first rank whose tension is recorded as maintainer question 1 and D97; production use waits for the answer or an `/api/v1/geocode` proxy. Never "the brief outranks the policy".

## Decision

Records plan row D97 and the M6 additions of D147, D148, D153, D163 (E4) and D166.

- `frontend/src/api/geocoder.ts` is deliberately apart from `api/client.ts`: the typed client retries 429s, which the policy forbids, so the geocoder does one `fetch`, no retry, no cache; the request carries the typed text alone (never the observer's coordinates, OBS-7), `format=jsonv2`, `limit=5`, `accept-language=<lang>`, `email=<value>` when `/meta.geocoder.email` gives one, and `referrerPolicy: 'strict-origin-when-cross-origin'` beside the `index.html` meta; `GEOCODER_POLICY_MIN_INTERVAL_MS = 1000` and `geocoderMinIntervalMs()` never let `/meta.geocoder.min_interval_ms` go below the policy's second.
- The observer panel enforces submit-only with one request in flight and at least `max(min_interval_ms, 1000)` between requests; 403 and 429 map to `blocked` (no automatic retry, the user is told), other failures to `unavailable`, `invalid` or `no_results`; a pick sets Earth at the place with `elev: 0`; the attribution text comes verbatim from `/meta.geocoder.attribution`; the disable toggle lives in memory, initialised from `/meta.geocoder.enabled`.
- M6: `connect-src 'self' https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org` in the web-tier CSP (`frontend/security-headers.ts`), the origin coming from `SKY_GEOCODER_ORIGIN`, which must equal the origin of `SKYAPI_GEOCODER_URL` (B-85; `e2e/csp.spec.ts` compares the served `connect-src` with `new URL(meta.geocoder.url).origin`, risk R104); `/meta.geocoder.email` is public by design (D166, one sentence in `.env.example` and `docs/api.md`); the `foreignRequests` fixture of `e2e/fixtures.ts` fails any request to another origin unless a spec calls `allow(host)` for the geocoder it routes itself; error path E4 (`e2e/observer.spec.ts` l.103): a routed 429 on the second submit shows the `geocoder.blocked` alert, exactly two requests in total, none more after 1.5 s.

## Consequences

- Tests and CI never contact Nominatim (l.428): every spec routes the geocoder; the CORS behaviour of the public instance is a one-off manual browser check, never a test (R64).
- The place search is not production-ready until question 1 is answered; the M7 release checklist carries that item beside the production `SKY_GEOCODER_ORIGIN` value.
- Only the typed text leaves the browser (OBS-7): the request carries no coordinates, and the `Referer` the policy asks for is the app origin alone under `strict-origin-when-cross-origin`.
- The data registry row 12 (`docs/data.md`) names the policy URL and the ODbL data license; the About screen renders the attribution.

## Revisit trigger

- Question 1 answered (browser-direct confirmed, or the proxy approved: then an `api_version` bump and a new ADR); the OSMF policy changes; a self-hosted instance or another provider configured through `/meta.geocoder` and `SKY_GEOCODER_ORIGIN`.
